What you can explore

This snapshot contains short inventories of 3,854 available full-text paper records. There are 3,699 included records, 137 exclusions and 18 records marked uncertain. Select a biological group, then a biomarker, to inspect reported measurement properties, paper titles and cited source passages. The paper-summary view retains excluded and uncertain records for inspection.

The view is organized into 50 biological systems and processes. Treg measurements share one display node across 89 paper records, while individual subsets and measured properties remain distinguishable. Fetuin A naming variants share one node across 10 paper records.

How the snapshot was made

Downloaded scientific articles, including text extracted from PDFs, were inventoried with DeepSeek. The inventory records study focus, a short summary, objective measurement names and properties, and references to text passages. The biological groups were subsequently curated with an assistant using the inventory, co-occurrence patterns, reusable name/context rules, selected summaries and source checks. Topic grouping did not use another DeepSeek pass.

The map accounts for 26,192 raw labels: 15,817 were assigned to groups, 1,137 were marked outside the biomarker scope, and 9,238 were left unresolved. These counts precede paper exclusions and display-family consolidation. After those presentation steps the viewer contains 24,949 nodes, including unresolved labels. Unmapped items remain visible in their own review view rather than receiving speculative assignments.

Twenty-five failed inventory responses were reviewed locally, and one accepted CBT study was corrected. Another 22 paper-level exclusions were recorded in the taxonomy. This public snapshot contains the reviewed results, not the API request history.

What the map does and does not say

  • Inventory stage: detailed extraction has not run. “Inventory only” does not mean a measurement is normal or that a study found no effect. Directions mentioned inside short summaries or properties are not a completed comparison-level extraction.
  • Shared names, distinct measurements: display families preserve original labels and properties. Tissue, subtype, assay and comparator can differ; no numerical effects have been pooled.
  • Scope: whole CBT/psychological and anxiety/depression intervention studies are excluded. Symptoms, questionnaire/cognitive-test scores and sex are not standalone biomarker nodes. Biomarker–symptom and biomarker–sex relationships are relevant when explicitly measured.
  • Solid branches show curated group membership. A membership is an organizational classification, not a mechanism established by every linked paper.
  • Dashed links show co-mentioned measurements within papers. The view retains the union of each biomarker’s 12 strongest neighbours and shows at most 12 links on the visible page. Co-occurrence is not causality or proof of a biological interaction.
  • Counts are paper records: preprints and later publications can describe the same study. Counts are not independent-cohort counts, study-quality scores or evidence weights.
  • Extraction and curation are imperfect: not every rare label was checked against an external ontology. Summaries can contain model errors. PDF conversion can miss scanned text, figures and table structure; large omics tables may not be fully inventoried. Source passages establish traceability, not correctness of interpretation.

Supporting text and data

The website includes the original viewer’s paper summaries, measurement labels and properties, decision reasons, co-occurrence links used in the view, and cited source text. Open “Read source passages” in the evidence panel or a paper summary. The downloadable topic map contains group definitions, assignments and their reasons, explicit unresolved labels, display families and curated exclusions. Full article PDFs, raw API responses and resumable analysis databases are not part of this website.

Group definitions and selected biological anchors were informed by the Reactome immune-system hierarchy, the Reactome process hierarchy, and the UniProt AHSG / fetuin-A entry. This was not a complete automated Reactome or UniProt annotation run.

Biological groups

Monocytes, macrophages & dendritic cells

Myeloid cell abundance, phenotype, antigen presentation and measured functions.

Neutrophils & extracellular traps

Neutrophil phenotypes, granulocyte activity and NET-related measurements.

Mast cells, histamine & allergic effectors

Mast cells, basophils, eosinophils, IgE and measured histamine/allergic mediators.

Innate sensing, inflammasomes & host defense

Pattern-recognition receptors, inflammasome activation and measured innate host-defense programs.

Complement

Classical, alternative and lectin-pathway components, activation products and functional assays.

NK cells & innate cytotoxicity

Natural-killer/innate lymphoid phenotypes, cytotoxic machinery and functional assays.

T-cell activation, differentiation & exhaustion

CD4/CD8 and other T-cell populations, activation/exhaustion markers and TCR-related measurements.

Regulatory T cells & immune tolerance

Treg abundance, subsets, phenotype and suppressive function. Distinct subsets remain identifiable within the shared display node.

B cells, plasma cells & immunoglobulins

B-cell/plasma-cell populations, antibody production, immunoglobulin classes and BCR repertoires.

Autoantibodies & autoreactivity

Measured self-reactive antibodies, receptor autoantibodies and autoreactivity assays; positivity does not establish pathogenicity.

Pathogen-specific immune responses

Antigen-specific antibodies, neutralization, and pathogen-specific lymphocyte responses. These are distinct from detecting the pathogen itself.

Type I/III interferons & antiviral programs

IFN-alpha/beta/lambda, their receptors and explicitly identified interferon-stimulated antiviral programs.

IFN-gamma, IL-12 & type 1 immunity

IFN-gamma/IL-12-related measurements and explicitly identified Th1 programs; membership does not imply a cell of origin.

IL-4/IL-5/IL-13 & type 2 immunity

Type 2 cytokine and explicitly identified Th2 measurements.

IL-17/IL-23 & Th17-associated biology

IL-17/IL-23-family measurements and explicitly identified Th17 programs.

IL-1, IL-6 & TNF inflammatory signaling

These cytokine families and their receptors/regulators. Antagonists and receptor levels retain their distinct identities.

IL-10, TGF-beta & regulatory cytokines

Regulatory/growth-factor cytokine measurements. Context-dependent effects are not reduced to a universal anti-inflammatory sign.

Chemokines & leukocyte trafficking

CC/CXC/CX3C/XC chemokines, chemokine receptors and measured cell-trafficking signals.

Acute-phase & systemic inflammatory markers

CRP, acute-phase proteins, sedimentation and broad blood inflammatory indices; these are nonspecific markers.

Endothelium, vascular barriers & angiogenesis

Endothelial activation, adhesion, glycocalyx, vascular junctions and angiogenic mediators.

Platelets, coagulation & fibrinolysis

Platelet biology, coagulation factors, fibrin/microclots and fibrinolytic measurements.

Erythrocytes, iron & oxygen carriage

Red-cell indices, hemoglobin, iron handling and related oxygen-carriage measurements.

Perfusion, microcirculation & oxygen delivery

Blood-flow, microvascular reactivity and tissue oxygenation measurements, kept distinct from arterial oxygen saturation and intrinsic mitochondrial respiration.

Autonomic function & orthostatic physiology

HRV, autonomic testing, orthostatic hemodynamics, sweating and related physiological readouts.

Cardiac function, injury & hemodynamics

Cardiac imaging, electrophysiology, injury markers and central hemodynamic measurements.

Pulmonary function, gas exchange & lung imaging

Spirometry, diffusion, ventilation, blood gases and measured lung structure/pathology.

Brain structure, activity & connectivity

Structural/functional brain imaging and central electrophysiology. Cognitive questionnaire/test scores are not standalone nodes.

Neuroimmune activity, glia & neural injury

Glial/neuronal injury markers, brain immune activity and barrier-related neural measurements.

Peripheral nerves, sensory & motor physiology

Peripheral nerve, small-fiber, neuromuscular and directly tested sensory measurements, including olfactory testing.

Sleep & circadian physiology

Instrumented sleep, circadian timing and measured activity/sleep rhythms; self-report sleep scales are excluded.

Skeletal muscle & exercise physiology

Muscle structure, force, body-composition assays and objective exercise capacity/CPET measurements. Performance endpoints remain distinguishable from molecular biomarkers.

Mitochondria & cellular bioenergetics

Respiration, oxidative phosphorylation, ATP synthesis, mitochondrial dynamics and intrinsic mitochondrial measurements.

Oxidative stress & antioxidant defenses

Measured reactive species, oxidation products, antioxidants and redox defense systems.

Glucose, insulin & central carbon metabolism

Glycemia/insulin regulation, glycolysis and central carbon substrates/enzymes, with context preserved.

Lipids, fatty acids & lipoproteins

Lipid classes, lipoprotein particles, lipid metabolism and fatty-acid measurements.

Amino acids & nitrogen metabolism

Amino-acid abundance, metabolism and nitrogen-related biochemical pathways.

Tryptophan, kynurenine & serotonin

Tryptophan catabolites, serotonin and explicitly measured pathway components; tissue and source remain separate.

Purines, pyrimidines & nucleotide metabolism

Nucleotide/nucleoside metabolites and their turnover/signaling measurements.

Microbiome composition & microbial metabolism

Measured microbial taxa, diversity, metagenomic functions and explicitly microbial metabolites. Not equivalent to viable infection.

Intestinal barrier & microbial translocation

Gut permeability, epithelial integrity and measured translocation products/responses.

Viral persistence, reactivation & pathogen detection

Pathogen nucleic acids/antigens, tissue detection and directly measured microbial infection. Serology alone remains in pathogen-specific immunity.

HPA axis & adrenal stress hormones

Cortisol, ACTH and measured adrenal/HPA-axis activity, with sampling time and assay context retained.

Thyroid, reproductive & other endocrine signaling

Thyroid, gonadal and other endocrine hormones/receptors and measured endocrine axes; sex itself is not a node.

Renal function, fluids & electrolytes

Renal injury/function, fluid regulation, ions and acid-base measurements.

Hepatic, digestive & nutritional biochemistry

Liver/pancreatic biochemistry, bile-related and nutrient-status measurements; BMI/anthropometric risk labels are excluded.

Extracellular matrix, fibrosis & mineralization

Matrix turnover, collagen/fibrosis, remodeling and mineralization regulators.

Cell stress, death, senescence & proteostasis

Apoptosis/other cell-death pathways, senescence, autophagy and protein stress responses.

Extracellular vesicles & intercellular cargo

Vesicle abundance, origin, physical characteristics and measured cargo; cargo identity remains visible.

Genetic susceptibility & epigenetic regulation

Variants, HLA alleles, methylation/chromatin and other measured genetic/epigenetic associations; these are not proof of causal genes.

RNA, protein expression & molecular regulation

Explicit RNA/protein expression programs, translation and signaling assays lacking a more specific biological assignment. Unidentified individual molecules are not forced into this group.
